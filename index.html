Summary - Key Observations

661 attendees in the funnel view, 582 transcripts in the call view, 16 FDE enrollments in attendee scope out of 27 total sales.
  1. 47% of leakage is Sales process, against 45% demand quality and 9% offer - rising to 52% inside the positive pipeline.
  2. 81% of calls end without a next step and 47% carry an unresolved objection. Enrolled leads had a next step 80% of the time. The most consistent gap in the dataset.
  3. 5-8 and 9-15 are near-identical on call quality - every dimension within five points except objection resolution, 7% against 16%.
  4. The 5-8 shortfall is a channel problem. T500: 131 leads, zero enrollments. Non-T500: 46 leads, 4 enrollments (8.7%). T500 calls reach pricing 33% of the time against 54%.
  5. The positive pipeline does not behave like buyers. Pricing 54% vs 87%, next step 28% vs 80%, urgency 13% vs 60%. The weekly number overstates real intent.
  6. Tiering separates buyers cleanly. 202 TOP / 157 MIDDLE / 205 LOW, with 14 of 15 enrolled in TOP. 68 TOP-tier leads sit in Not Interested - written off too early.
  7. 231 leads are recoverable now - 14 P0 and 217 P1, plus 213 on nurture. Only 120 are genuinely dead.
  8. Intent, not price, is the terminal blocker. "No plan" is 37-39% of connected leads; pricing objection is 2-6%.

H · Data quality flags

FLAG 1
Connect-to-DCD is inflated. DCD comes from a CRM status field, Connected from call logs, so they do not share a denominator. 84 attendees carry DCD_Flag = 1 with zero logged two-way calls; 56 of those have a DCD_Date predating their webinar, carried forward from an earlier programme cycle. This produces the five rows above 100% in §10.
Current figures show a 27 point gap between 5-8 (65%) and 16-20 (92%). The clean definition DCD_Flag = 1 AND Connected = 1 gives 55% and 62% - a 7 point gap, and drops DCD from 340 to 256. Clean rates: 3-5 60%, 5-8 55%, 9-15 68%, 16-20 62%, 20+ 52%. Do not quote the 27 point gap externally. Applying the clean definition weakens the cohort story and strengthens the execution story.
Fix at source: stamp DCD_Date only on a call logged after the current webinar, or add a DCD_source field so carried-forward tags stay distinguishable.
FLAG 2
Two Enrolled numbers exist. Every figure outside §5 is FDE-only. By cohort, FDE-only against all-programme: 5-8 2.3% vs 2.8%, 9-15 2.7% vs 5.8%, 16-20 3.3% vs 9.9%, 20+ 2.0% vs 10.0%, overall 2.4% vs 5.6%. The 21 non-FDE enrollments are mostly Transformative GenAI and AgenticAI variants; 16 of them have a blank course field and cannot be verified from the sheet. Never place both rates on one chart without labels.
Worth a separate look: 16-20 converts at 9.9% across all programmes but 3.3% into FDE. That band is buying, just not buying FDE.
FLAG 3
Meaningful calls exceed connects in every cohort. 3-5 29 vs 25, 5-8 128 vs 99, 9-15 187 vs 161, 16-20 80 vs 64, 20+ 33 vs 25. A call over two minutes should be a subset of a connect, so this is the same denominator mismatch as flag 1 - talk-time records against a call-log flag. Treat the meaningful-call rate as directional and do not compute a meaningful-calls-per-connect ratio from it.
FLAG 4
cube5.json carries a stale enrollment count. Its enr field totals 21 (3-5 0, 5-8 4, 9-15 10, 16-20 4, 20+ 3) against the 16 shown here, and the per-webinar splits differ. The dashboard tables are correct and were updated after the FDE-only reconciliation; the JSON was not. Rebuild enrollment from the sales tracker, not from cube5.json. derived.json also holds only three cohort keys (3-5, 5-8, 9+), so §5’s five-band split cannot be reproduced from it directly.
FLAG 5
RTE_Flag is historical and is not a subset of Positive. Of the 67 RTE-flagged leads in the transcript set, only 21 currently sit in POSITIVE. 26 are in NOT_INTERESTED, 12 in ENROLLED_OTHER, 7 in ENROLLED and 1 in OPEN_STUCK. The flag records that a lead reached Ready to Enroll at some point and survives a later status change, so it cannot be added into Positive as a count without double-counting leads that have since moved on. §5 merges the Ready to enroll status into Positive, which is clean; §2 keeps the flag as a labelled companion row.
Fix at source: keep RTE_Flag for history and add a current-stage field, or stamp RTE_cleared_date when a lead leaves the stage.

582 of 591 transcripts analysed - 9 were dropped by the batch process and not recovered. 3-5 is attendees only, and its 0.0% reflects 20% never dialled at 3.9 attempts per lead, so it is not a clean cohort verdict. Transcripts cover calls from 27 July 2026 onward; earlier cycles are out of scope by design.
